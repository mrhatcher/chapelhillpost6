Happening Now

Active Programs & Events

These are the regular activities happening at Post 6 right now. All members and guests are welcome.

WED

Wednesday Socials

Every Wednesday, members and guests gather for food, drinks, and fellowship. It's the easiest way to get to know Post 6. No RSVP needed — just show up at the hall.

Every Wednesday at the Post

2nd

Monthly Post Meetings

On the 2nd Tuesday of every month, members meet for business, updates, and fellowship. 5:30 PM Social, 6:30 PM Dinner, 7:30 PM Official Meeting. Guests and prospective members welcome.

2nd Tuesday, monthly

SAL

SAL Pancake Breakfasts

The Sons of the American Legion host regular pancake breakfasts open to the community. Great food, great company, and all proceeds support veteran programs and scholarships.

Monthly — check calendar for dates

JAM

Marathon Jam

Annual music fundraiser raising money for the Fisher House at Fort Bragg, which provides free lodging for families of hospitalized veterans. Post 6's goal: $10,000.

Annual event

VSO

Free Veterans Services

VSO William Munsee helps veterans and families navigate VA benefits, disability claims, pension applications, and healthcare enrollment — all at no charge. Contact vso@chapelhillpost6.com.

Ongoing — by appointment

HW

Healing Waters Fly Fishing

Project Healing Waters offers fly-fishing therapy for wounded and disabled veterans and active military, in partnership with the VA Medical Center in Durham. Free equipment, instruction, and outings.

Ongoing program

Our Programs

Post Organizations & Programs

These are the core organizations that define Post No. 6's commitment to veterans, families, and the community.

AUX

American Legion Auxiliary

Open to spouses, daughters, granddaughters, and other female relatives of veterans. The Auxiliary is one of the largest patriotic service organizations for women in the world.

SAL

Sons of the American Legion (SAL)

Male descendants of veterans are eligible to join the SAL — continuing the Legion's legacy across generations while serving the community.

ALR

American Legion Riders

Motorcycle enthusiasts who raise funds and awareness for veteran causes. The Riders participate in parades, charity runs, and honor escort missions.

505

Boy Scout Troops 505 & 835

Post No. 6 proudly sponsors two active Boy Scout troops in Chapel Hill, helping shape the next generation of leaders through outdoor adventure and civic service.

B/G

Boys & Girls State / Oratorical Contest

We fund scholarships for high schoolers to attend Boys and Girls State leadership programs, and host the American Legion Oratorical Contest each year.

BD

Community Blood Drives

Post No. 6 hosts community blood drives throughout the year, partnering with local blood banks to ensure a stable supply for hospitals in the Chapel Hill area.

Service & Recognition

Honor Programs

Additional programs that showcase Post No. 6's commitment to service, recognition, and community engagement.

Honor Guard

An elite ceremonial unit recognized as the "Gold Standard" of Department of North Carolina Honor Guards. The Honor Guard performs at military funerals, dedications, post meetings, and community events. All Post No. 6 members are eligible and encouraged to join.

Post 6 Cruisers

A vehicle enthusiast group connected to the Post, bringing together members who share a passion for classic and specialty automobiles. The Cruisers support veteran outreach and community events, using their gatherings to raise visibility for the Post and its mission.

Flag Disposal Ceremonies

Post No. 6 conducts respectful flag retirement ceremonies for worn or damaged American flags. Bring your worn flag to any Post meeting and we will ensure it is retired with the dignity it deserves.

School Supply Drives

Post 6 organizes school supply drives to support local students and families in the Chapel Hill area, ensuring kids have the materials they need to succeed in the classroom.

Gold Standard Newsletter

The Post's newsletter keeps members informed about upcoming events, meetings, programs, and community news. The Gold Standard is published regularly and distributed to all active members.
