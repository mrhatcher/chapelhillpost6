A Growing Post — Cardinal Award Recognition

Post 6 has been recognized with both the Cardinal Award and Early Bird Award for outstanding membership growth and renewal. Our modern facility, active programs, and welcoming community continue to draw new members from Chapel Hill, Durham, Burlington, and surrounding areas.

As former Post Commander Lee Heavlin put it: we want to give every veteran a home. A larger, stronger Post means more resources to serve veterans in Chapel Hill and Orange County — whether you're a member or not.

Contact 1st Vice Commander Cliff Baldwin for membership questions: 1stvicecommander@alpost6.org

Who Can Be a Member?

Congress has broadened eligibility. If you served even one day on active federal service since December 7, 1941 and received an honorable discharge — you qualify.

AL

Legion Member

Any honorably discharged veteran who served on active federal service since Dec. 7, 1941. All branches welcome — Army, Navy, Marines, Air Force, Coast Guard, Space Force.

$60 / year
AUX

American Legion Auxiliary

Spouses, daughters, granddaughters, sisters, mothers, and grandmothers of eligible veterans. One of the largest women's patriotic organizations in the world.

Contact Us
SAL

Sons of the American Legion

Male descendants (sons, grandsons, great-grandsons, stepsons, and adopted sons) of members or deceased veterans eligible for Legion membership.

$25 / year

Ready to Continue Your Service?

We're not here to pressure anyone. We want members who are ready to give back a little to their community. If that's you — we'd love to have you.
