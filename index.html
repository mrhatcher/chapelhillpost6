Our Purpose

Standing on Four Pillars of Service

Since 1919, the American Legion has been guided by four core commitments. Post No. 6 lives these values every day in Chapel Hill and Orange County.

01
Veterans Affairs & Rehabilitation
Ensuring every veteran receives the care and benefits they earned.
02
National Security
Supporting a strong, well-prepared national defense.
03
Americanism
Promoting patriotism, youth programs, and civic responsibility.
04
Children & Youth
Investing in the next generation through scouting, Boys State, and scholarships.

Programs & Organizations

From youth development to veteran support, there's a place for every veteran and family member at Post No. 6.

AUX

American Legion Auxiliary

Open to spouses, daughters, granddaughters, and other female relatives of veterans. The Auxiliary is one of the largest patriotic service organizations for women in the world.

Learn more →
SAL

Sons of the American Legion (SAL)

Male descendants of veterans are eligible to join the SAL — continuing the Legion's legacy across generations while serving the community.

Learn more →
ALR

American Legion Riders

Motorcycle enthusiasts who raise funds and awareness for veteran causes. The Riders participate in parades, charity runs, and honor escort missions.

Learn more →
505

Boy Scout Troops 505 & 835

Post No. 6 proudly sponsors two active Boy Scout troops in Chapel Hill, helping shape the next generation of leaders through outdoor adventure and civic service.

Learn more →
B/G

Boys & Girls State / Oratorical Contest

We fund scholarships for high schoolers to attend Boys and Girls State leadership programs, and host the American Legion Oratorical Contest each year.

Learn more →
HW

Healing Waters & Veteran Services

Operation Healing Waters offers fly-fishing therapy to wounded warriors. Our Veteran Services Officer helps members navigate VA benefits and claims.
