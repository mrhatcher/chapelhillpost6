Every Week & Month

Regular Weekly & Monthly Activities

These recurring activities are the heartbeat of Post 6. No special signup needed — just come to the Post.

Wednesday Socials

Every Wednesday at the Post. Food, drinks, and fellowship. Members and guests welcome. The easiest way to get to know Post 6.

Monthly Post Meetings

2nd Tuesday of every month. 5:30 PM Social, 6:30 PM Dinner, 7:30 PM Official Meeting. All members welcome; guests and prospective members encouraged to attend.

SAL Pancake Breakfasts

Regular Saturday morning pancake breakfasts hosted by the Sons of the American Legion. Open to the public. Check the calendar for specific dates.

PEC Meetings

Post Executive Committee meets monthly to handle post business and planning. Check calendar for dates.

Legion Riders & SAL Meetings

Monthly meetings for the American Legion Riders and Sons of the American Legion chapters.

Community Blood Drives

Regular blood drives throughout the year partnering with local blood banks. Walk-ins always welcome.

Come Meet Us

Every Second Tuesday of the Month

Our monthly meetings are open to all members — come early to socialize, stay for dinner, and participate in the official meeting. Guests and prospective members are always welcome.
